# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Two people in the same household (Ethan + wife). They check in periodically — likely on mobile — to see where money is going, how savings are tracking, and whether they're on budget for the month. They already know their financial situation; they need a shared tool that keeps them in sync without friction. Power users, not beginners learning to budget.

## Product Purpose

Rov is a private household budgeting app for two. It tracks monthly budget categories, income sources, savings pods, assets, debts, and net worth over time. It is not a bank, not a public product, not a startup — it's a tool built for one household. Success looks like both people opening it, seeing the same numbers, and feeling oriented about their money.

## Brand Personality

Warm, encouraging, personal. Like a tool you built for your own family. Quiet and focused, never flashy. The name Rov (Hebrew: רוב — plenty, abundance) carries generosity and fullness, not austerity or anxiety. References: Linear's precision and restraint; Notion's spaciousness and readability.

## Anti-references

- **Mint / generic banking apps** — corporate, over-friendly, generic green-blue, feature bloat
- **Consumer fintech (Robinhood, Cash App)** — gamified, flashy, metric-hero cards, artificially energetic
- **Enterprise dashboards (Tableau, Power BI)** — data-dense, overwhelming, built for analysts not households
- **AI cream/sand default** — warm-beige near-white that reads as "Claude made this"; Rov already has a committed dark-navy identity

## Design Principles

1. **Shared clarity** — two people, one picture. Every screen should orient both of you, not just one.
2. **Honest numbers** — no soft-pedaling reality. Over budget is red. Good is good. Numbers are what they are.
3. **Quiet confidence** — stable and trustworthy without being loud. Restraint in chrome; warmth in tone.
4. **Built for returning users** — not optimized for onboarding or marketing. Assumed you know how it works.
5. **Personal, not generic** — this tool is yours. It can reference your household's real names, categories, and patterns.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum. Mobile-first (both users likely on iPhone). 16px minimum input font-size to prevent iOS zoom. Reduced motion support where animations are added.
